## English — Requirements and changes

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4+, Java 17. Universal JAR; ZoneCore 2.1.0+ required only on server. Optional ZoneCrates supplies the wallet for currency purchases; free/permission access works without it. Upgrade both sides from 1.10.x/1.11.x to protocol 9.

1.12.1 includes the wing expansion and 20-piece Anime collection: 94 default cosmetics, 61 geometric models and 14 wing styles. The redesigned wardrobe offers search/filter/sort, preview rotation, confirmed purchases and three saved outfits. New entries migrate additively and start permission-locked. The camera patch automatically hides your own aura in first person, clears lingering own particles when switching camera, and restores emission in third person; remote auras and wardrobe previews remain available. Same universal JAR, protocol 9, Core 2.1.0+ server-only. Client 1.12.1 was checked against server 1.12.0; earlier protocol 8 is incompatible. Preserve catalog, permissions, ownership and saved outfits.

Open `/cosmetic`, choose a category and preview an item. Confirm purchases separately; preview never grants ownership. Use Conjuntos to save/apply one of three outfits. No setting is needed for first-person aura protection.

## Collection and wardrobe / Colección y armario

The 1.11 wing expansion retained six existing IDs and added six. The 1.12 Anime collection adds four head items, four hands, four back accessories, four auras and four walking effects. The previous 74 entries remain, for 94 standard cosmetics. The 20 new entries are initially locked. /1.11 conservó seis alas y añadió seis;1.12 añade cuatro piezas de cada grupo indicado. Conserva74 anteriores y alcanza 94; las 20 nuevas empiezan bloqueadas.

Search supports name, ID and rarity; filters show all, available, for-sale and equipped entries. Sorting supports name, rarity and price. Preview can rotate or combine selection with current equipment but never buys or equips by itself. Purchase and outfit overwrite require confirmation. / Búsqueda, filtros, orden, giro y conjunto son visuales hasta confirmar la acción; compra y sobrescritura requieren confirmación.

Pending mutation buttons remain blocked across tabs. After eight seconds, Revisar estado requests status only without replaying a purchase. Closing the wardrobe is respected by late responses. New catalog revisions invalidate stale confirmations. / Acciones pendientes siguen bloqueadas; a los ocho segundos Revisar estado solo consulta. Respuestas tardías no reabren pantalla; revisión nueva invalida confirmación antigua.

## Camera patch / Parche de cámara

In first person your own aura geometry and particles are hidden; switching from third person also clears lingering own aura particles before their next visible frame. Third-person emission resumes normally. Remote auras, the wardrobe preview, other categories and unrelated world particles retain their behavior. / Primera persona oculta aura propia y limpia residuos al volver desde tercera; tercera restaura emisión. Auras ajenas, vista previa, otras categorías y partículas del mundo se conservan.

1.12.1 retains protocol 9 and was tested with a 1.12.0 server. A server already on 1.12.0 need not restart solely for this camera patch; each client needs 1.12.1 for the correction. Updating from older protocol versions requires compatible versions on both sides. / Un servidor 1.12.0 no necesita reiniciar solo por el parche; cada cliente sí requiere 1.12.1. Desde protocolos anteriores hay que actualizar ambos lados.

## Installation / Instalación

Stop the affected instance, back up world/configuration and replace only the previous same-mod/same-side JAR. Keep one JAR per mod per instance and preserve player data. / Detén la instancia correspondiente, respalda mundo/configuración y sustituye solo el JAR del mismo mod y lado. Conserva un JAR por mod e instancia y los datos de jugador.
